Official Event Information

What is
BEEF Con?

BEEF Con is an annual three-day gathering of highly trained gaming personnel, competitive specialists, and individuals willing to sacrifice an entire weekend to achieve absolutely nothing of measurable value.

The Mission

Bring the squad together. Bring the computers. Compete across multiple games. Win trophies. Eat an unreasonable amount of food. Establish bragging rights for the next twelve months.

The Summit

Hosted annually in Camas, Washington, BEEF Con combines competitive gaming, group activities, awards, food, friendship, and several traditions that have somehow survived multiple years of organizational review.

Historical Record

The Origin
of BEEF

In the beginning, there was Call of Duty: Black Ops. There was a clan tag. The clan tag was BEEF.

What began as a completely arbitrary four-letter designation eventually became the shared identity of a group of gamers. The designation survived multiple games, multiple years, and an increasingly unnecessary level of organizational infrastructure.

Eventually, someone decided that everyone should bring their computers to a house in Washington and spend an entire weekend competing against each other.

There is no meaning behind BEEF.
There never was.
There doesn't need to be.
