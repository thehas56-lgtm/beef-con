About the Summit

What is BEEF Con?

BEEF Con is an annual three-day gathering of BEEF-affiliated competitors, bringing together PC gaming, competition, food, friendship, and an unreasonable amount of organizational infrastructure.

Hosted in Camas, Washington, BEEF Con brings together approximately 15–20 invitees for a weekend of competitive gaming, group play, prizes, food, awards, and other officially sanctioned BEEF activities.

BEEF Archives

The Origin of BEEF

Before there was BEEF Con, there was simply BEEF.

In the early days of Call of Duty: Black Ops, a four-letter clan tag appeared in front of a gamertag simply because it looked funny.

There was no secret acronym. No elaborate meaning. No ancient gaming tradition.

And yet, the tag stuck.

Over time, BEEF became a symbol of a group of gamers who could come together across different games and compete as a squad.

Eventually, the clan tag became a tradition.

And then, inevitably, it became a convention.

There is no meaning behind BEEF.
